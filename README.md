# In Pay

<sub>This project was developed for the Hackadev event, part of the [Sharpcoders](https://imatech.io/programas/sharp-coders) program, by Team #Curiouscat.</sub>

Welcome to **In Pay** — your simplified digital bank and fintech solution.
Built by developers, for developers, our intuitive platform streamlines payment integrations while functioning as a secure digital banking system.

Ready to simplify your financial operations?
